# Notice

## The advisors are simulations

The council seats are simulations built from the public words of real people (Andy Grove, Reed Hastings, Elon Musk, Jeff Bezos, Bill Gates, Satya Nadella, and others named in `roster.json`). They are not those people, are not affiliated with or endorsed by them or their companies, and do not speak for them. Their names are used only to identify whose public reasoning each seat is modeled on. The healthcare translations in each brain are inference, because few of these leaders spoke about running a hospital.

## Quotations in brains/

Each brain stores short verbatim quotations (each under about 45 words) from the person's public letters, essays, talks, testimony, and interviews, with a source id that links to the original URL in that brain's `sources.md`. They are included for commentary, criticism, and educational analysis of management ideas. Copyright in the quoted material remains with its authors and publishers. No full texts are included; the raw downloads used to build the brains (`build/`) are excluded from this repository.

If you hold rights in quoted material and want it removed or changed, open an issue on this repository and it will be addressed promptly.

## Not professional advice

Council verdicts are decision support for administrative and business questions. They are not legal, financial, or clinical advice. The Governance Guardian seat names laws and standards to check, not to rely on; confirm with your institution's legal, procurement, and compliance offices. The skill is out of scope for diagnosis, treatment, or any patient-level decision.
