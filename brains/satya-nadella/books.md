# Satya Nadella: Books

Only what he says in these sources. Where he names a book or thinker, the entry records how he uses it.

## Mindset (Carol Dweck)

The idea behind his culture reset. He calls it the meme that changed Microsoft. He says he was lucky to find it.

- "I must say I was lucky enough to pick the meme of growth mindset from Carol Dweck’s work and it’s done wonders." [S011]
- "it's really helped us go from this, know it all to learn it alls" [S009]

**Why it worked.** It did not feel like a new CEO's rule. It spoke to people as people, at home and at work.

- "it is not considered new dogma from a new CEO. It is something that I think spoke to people as humans." [S010]
- "It helps us be better parents, better partners, better colleagues, neighbors, and leaders." [S010]
- "People felt like this is a meme that made them better leaders, it made them better human beings." [S011]

**How it spread.** He did not plan every step. It grew on its own once leaders made it personal.

- "not because we really thought it all through, it took on, as I said, some organic life" [S011]

**How he reads it.** The work is to face your own fixed mindset. Naming it in others misses the point (inferred from S011). The letters link it to the culture gap and to core values (inferred from S005, S006).

- "Culture transformation is a continuous process of learning, renewal, and having the everyday courage to confront our own fixed mindsets, while remaining true to our enduring values of respect, integrity, and accountability." [S005]

## Nietzsche and Rilke (closing the 2014 memo)

He closes his first big employee memo with lines from Nietzsche and Rilke. He uses them to turn strategy into a personal call to courage (inferred from S001). His own words around them:

- "Even more important, we must have courage in the face of opportunity." [S001]
- "With the courage to transform individually, we will collectively transform this company and seize the great opportunity ahead." [S001]

## Other thinkers he credits

**Reid Hoffman on refounding.** He credits Hoffman for the idea that companies refound themselves (inferred from S010).

- "companies also go through these refounding moments" [S010]

**Elad Gil's AI safety taxonomy.** He accepts a three-level split: harms today, physical-world risk, and existential risk (inferred from S010).

**Bezos.** He credits Bezos with the idea of being fine with being misunderstood (inferred from S010).

**Bill Gates on natural language.** "Bill has been obsessed about natural language" [S011]

## His father's diary (a personal learning text)

Not a book, but the closest thing to one in these sources. He took his learning system from it.

- "There's a thing that I look at as my learning system. If there is one, I picked it up from my dad." [S009]
- "tasks, done. People met. Ideas generated to act on." [S009]
