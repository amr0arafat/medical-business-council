# Satya Nadella: Principles

Short rules he states directly. One line each: the principle, then the quote and source.

## Mission and purpose

- Purpose anchors every decision. "every one of us who work in any company need that anchoring in order to then go on to make all the decisions and work we do" [S009]
- Technology is never the goal. "But it's not about technology for technology's sake!" [S002]
- Purpose lives in the customer's success. "Our sense of purpose lies in our customers' success." [S004]
- Solve problems, do not create them. "As a corporation, our purpose and actions must be aligned with addressing the world's problems, not creating new ones." [S007]
- Innovation counts only as delivered value. "The innovation we have driven over the past year matters only if we translate it into enduring value for our customers." [S008]
- Work should carry meaning. "I truly believe that we spend far too much time at work for it not to drive personal meaning and satisfaction." [S001]
- Health comes before the economy. "COVID-19 has underscored that without a healthy society, we cannot sustain a healthy economy." [S006]

## Culture and change

- Culture is the root of decisions. "Ultimately, we will only achieve our mission if we live our culture. It is at the root of every decision we make." [S006]
- Change starts with each person. "And culture change starts with one individual at a time." [S001]
- Put everything on the table. "Nothing is off the table in how we think about shifting our culture to deliver on this core strategy." [S001]
- Pair change with learning. "we know the changes above will bring on the need for new training, learning and experimentation" [S001]
- Courage is personal. "We must each have the courage to transform as individuals." [S001]
- Do not wait to be asked. "the best career advice I got is don't wait for your next job to do your best work" [S009]
- Hope needs work. "But helplessness is corrosive to hope, and hope plus work is what is required." [S006]
- Relearn when the game changes. "we’ve got to relearn everything because there’s a complete new game in town again" [S011]
- Change takes patience. "you have to have patience" [S011]
- Do not ask others to carry all the risk. "it’s easy to talk about risk because you want the other person to take risk" [S011]

## Growth mindset

- Admit imperfection to close the gap. "It's not by claiming a growth mindset but by knowing that we are imperfect but can learn and get better that we can close the gap between our espoused culture and the lived experience for every employee at the company." [S006]
- Keep confronting the fixed mindset. "For us, that means constantly exercising our growth mindset and confronting our fixed mindset" [S008]
- Do not get comfortable with success. "But it's easy to talk about what we're doing well." [S007]
- Stay relevant and measurable. "the test of any brand is its ability to have measurable impact and to remain relevant over time" [S003]
- Compete, do not admire. "Compete, don’t admire." [S011]

## Breaking silos and customer obsession

- Customers are everyone's job. "Obsessing over our customers is everybody's job." [S001]
- Information must not sit in silos. "We don't have time for the information we need personally and professionally to live in different silos." [S003]
- Meet needs people cannot yet state. "listening and then innovating to meet customers' unarticulated needs" [S005]
- Teaming beats star teams. "great teams are important, but great teaming is the currency" [S009]
- Flatten to move ideas. "We will increase the fluidity of information and ideas by taking actions to flatten the organization and develop leaner business processes." [S001]
- Push accountability down. "In making these changes we are getting closer to the customer and pushing more accountability throughout the organization." [S001]

## Empathy and care

- Know each person's life. "figuring out what is the lived experience of the people in your team" [S009]
- Make sure every voice is heard. "being able to deeply have empathy for that and then making sure that their voice is heard in a meeting" [S009]
- Assume people carry hidden strain. "everybody is going through some tail event" [S009]
- Well-being is productivity. "well-being is one of the most important pieces of productivity" [S009]
- Care is management, not a tool. "It's not about a tool, but it's good old fashioned good management practices that we need in order for people to have their wellbeing taken care of." [S009]
- The manager owns onboarding. "the full service concierge is now your manager and unless your manager cares and takes the responsibility of onboarding you" [S009]
- Small leader habits set norms. "That's the shortest way to destroy a whole bunch of people's weekend" [S009]
- Fix systems, not people. "But I got to really look at the systemic issues here and then help them recognize to fix it." [S009]

## Leadership

- Leaders and tech go together. "leadership and management and technological excellence, both go together." [S009]
- Measure leaders by their successors. "if the person who takes over from me in any role is able to succeed, then maybe you did something right" [S010]
- Do not claim the credit. "I'm very suspect of anybody who comes in and says, oh, I showed up at the job." [S010]
- Invest early. "you got to invest like long before it's conventional wisdom. You can't expect markets to always reward you." [S010]
- Control the organic plan first. "First focus on having a great organic plan because that's the one thing you can always control." [S010]
- Fewer people in decisions, more accountability. "You will see fewer people get involved in decisions and more emphasis on accountability." [S001]

## Technology and adoption

- Every organization becomes a tech organization. "Today, every company is a technology company, and every organization will increasingly need to build its own proprietary technology solutions to compete and grow." [S005]
- Tech must reach every process. "Every organization in every industry will need to infuse technology into every business process and function so they can do more with less." [S007]
- Tech is deflationary. "Technology is a deflationary force in an inflationary economy." [S007]
- Digital capability builds resilience. "those fortified by digital technology are more resilient and more capable of transforming when faced with sweeping changes like those we are experiencing" [S006]
- Learning must happen inside work. "learning in the flow of work will be increasingly important" [S006]
- Skills are not enough. "But skills alone aren't enough" [S007]
- Value decides AI's worth. "It all depends on how much economic surplus you can create using this technology." [S010]
- Attention is the scarce resource. "the true scarce commodity is increasingly human attention" [S001]
- Expertise is now within reach. "Where the internet era put information at our fingertips, AI is putting expertise at our fingertips." [S008]

## Trust, security, responsible AI

- Trust is earned. "It is something we must earn." [S008]
- Privacy is a right. "We believe privacy is a fundamental human right." [S005]
- Customers own their data. "customers own their own data" [S005]
- Transparency underpins trust. "transparency is foundational to trust" [S006]
- Build security in. "we build security by design into every product we sell" [S007]
- Security needs partners. "In an increasingly complex world, technology alone is not enough to combat increasing threats. It also requires partnerships, both with governments and industries." [S006]
- Guard against side effects. "we must also guard against the unintended consequences of AI" [S005]
- Norms are a public choice. "it's a societal choice. It's not any one company can do everything here." [S010]

## Partnership

- A partnership is not a press release. "don't think of it as a press release" [S010]
- Do not trap partners. "No customer wants to be dependent on a provider that sells them technology on one end and competes with them on the other." [S005]
- Stay open. "The Microsoft Cloud is open and offers choice." [S003]
- No firm solves big problems alone. "No single company is going to solve macro challenges like climate change alone" [S005]
