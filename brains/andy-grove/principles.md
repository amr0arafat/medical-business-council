# Andy Grove: principles

Short rules Grove states directly. One line each.

## Output and measurement

- Judge managers by results. "Effective managers are distinguished by what they accomplish, not by what they are." [S013]
- Argue from evidence, not feeling. "Don't argue with the emotions, argue with the data." [S003]
- Trust the data over the authority. "Look at the data! It says there’s the surface charge." [S001]
- Remember data look backward. "the data that deal with your business are pertinent to your past, and don't say anything about your future." [S003]
- Do not use data as cover. "You can always hide behind the data." [S003]
- Check the instrument before trusting the reading. "So I also decided to test the tests." [S007]
- Do the arithmetic yourself. "Can we afford all this? Let's do the math." [S006]
- A fast part is only as good as the slow one. "Processor performance is only as useful as the I/O allows it to be." [S004]
- Compare across all methods, not within one. "The right answer, in my view, can be arrived at only by comparing the results achieved by the practitioners of all the different treatment forms." [S007]
- Do not take one recommendation as final. "Investigate things, come to your own conclusions, don’t take any one recommendation as gospel." [S007]

## Priorities and resources

- Every yes needs a no. "When I say the yeses have to equals the nos, that's what I mean." [S003]
- Fix specific things first. "Fix specific problems of the system." [S006]
- Limit the program list. "I propose that you commit to two - and only two - programs." [S006]
- Set a standard and enforce it. "The same goes for acceptable patient waiting times: Set the standard, and implement it." [S006]
- Ask for concrete commitments now. "Commit to doing these two concrete things - now." [S006]
- Scaling is the real work. "Scaling is hard work but necessary to make innovation matter." [S008]
- Price to the market. "you must price the market and cost must follow price." [S003]
- Do not add complexity. "I think we have a tendency toward adding imaginary complexities to a problem which is already unimaginably complicated." [S012]
- Do not plan on a hoped-for recovery. "Anybody who counts on that kind of a thing as a strategy element is living a fairly unreasonable and unexamined strategy life." [S003]

## Speed and action

- Act before the turn, not after. "if you want to prosper on the other side of the Strategic Inflection Point, you must take action before you get there." [S002]
- Move fast. "it is the very key in all of this to strive toward making these changes as fast as possible because time is your ally." [S003]
- Act early and decisively. "the only chance we have to reverse the deterioration is if we act early and decisively." [S008]
- Use your best shot first. "I think you should hit a tumor with what you believe is your best shot, early and hard." [S007]
- Do not waste an early signal. "What PSA gives you is the chance to act early. Don’t blow it." [S007]
- Engage, then plan. "Engage and then plan. And get it better." [S012]
- Accept rough first versions. "be quick and dirty" [S012]
- Act before you announce. "It is important to put the actions in place before you start talking about it" [S003]
- Shape change while you still can. "And the time to shape the future is while we are at that Inflection Point before those two curves have diverged." [S002]
- Choose change or have it chosen for you. "If we want to remain a leading economy, we change on our own, or change will continue to be forced upon us." [S008]

## People, candor, and culture

- Listen to bad news. "It is extremely important to be able to listen to the people who bring you bad news" [S003]
- Remove fear or lose your warnings. "Unless you deal with this fear, unless you live this fear you will never hear from those helpful Cassandras" [S003]
- Think for yourself. "Let’s think for ourselves." [S012]
- Seniors notice change last. "those of us in senior management are among the last ones to notice." [S003]
- No magic manager traits. "I advise aspiring managers to stop worrying about what magic characteristics they need" [S013]
- Use the strengths people have. "The trick is to use what you're good at and acquire some skill in other areas and make that work for you." [S013]
- Be serious about work, not about yourself. "Don't take yourself seriously, take your work seriously." [S013]
- Stay approachable. "If you can't banter, who is going to approach you with a difficult problem?" [S013]
- Openness feeds teamwork. "Communication begets collaboration." [S013]
- Own your conduct; do not wait for the top. "We all have a responsibility for our own actions." [S013]
- Coordination beats individual freedom when you want a result. "his freedom renders your freedom useless." [S001]
- Bad examples teach too. "I learned a lot about management with a multiplier of minus one." [S001]
- Empowerment is hard in practice. "You want to do it and in reality, when you do it, it is hell." [S003]
- People resist change aimed at them. "We love change when it happens to somebody else" [S003]

## Strategy and institutions

- A strategic inflection point demands a real change. "Nothing less is sufficient." [S003]
- Ask if the cheap option is good enough. "The question to ask is: Is it good enough?" [S003]
- Not every cheap rival is a threat. "Not everything that's low cost is a Segment Zero." [S003]
- Win on your own terms. "we not only had to win; we had to win our way." [S012]
- What can be built will be built. "Anything that can be done in technology will be done." [S002]
- Grow the whole market. "We want the whole pie to grow." [S011]
- Governance is strategy. "After all, good strategy is good governance." [S010]
- Directors must be tied to results. "For them to own a piece of the action, they need to be visibly associated with the outcome" [S010]
- Continuity makes a good board. "Continuity is the key" [S010]
- Give public vehicles an exit. "Then let this organization license the manufacturing technology to private companies and let it go out of business." [S009]
- Broaden the definition of the business. "We need to broaden our thinking about what it is we do and how we do it." [S005]
- Look back ten years to see ten ahead. "To see what can happen in the next ten years, look at what has happened in the last ten years." [S007]

## Health care

- Treat your health like your money. "If you look after your investments, I think you should look after your life as well." [S007]
- Expect specialist bias. "There is no good gatekeeper in this business." [S007]
- Patients are ahead of providers, so respond. "So if nothing else, self-defense compels you to take some action." [S002]
- Ask for outcomes evidence on any intervention. "Have you studied the impact of these electronic discussion groups on their prognosis and the well-being of the patients?" [S002]
- Do not put a middleman in a direct channel. "this is utter nonsense and it throws out all the benefits of the immediacy of e-mail." [S002]
- Keep the record simple and do it now. "this is a technically trivial job if you act now." [S006]
