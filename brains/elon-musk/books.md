# Elon Musk: books and thinkers

The notes contain few book references. The sources are essays, testimony, talks, a podcast, a press conference and articles. None of them is a reading list. This file records only what the notes show.

## Thinkers and works he cites

**Tom Friedman (named by him, 2025-05-30).** At the DOGE farewell press conference he credited a spending maxim to "the great Tom Friedman" [S008]. The source transcript gives the name this way, and it is kept as transcribed.
- "money is spent most poorly when it is someone else's money being spent on people you don't know" [S008]
- Use: the maxim underpins his view that public budgets drift toward waste (inferred from S008).

**Military history (reading habit, 2023-11-09).** He describes reading military history to find root causes.
- "trying to figure out what really was the cause of victory in any particular case as opposed to what one side or another claim the reason" [S007]
- No specific titles are named in the notes.

**Physics as a thinking tool (2013, 2023).** Not a book, but the discipline he cites most as his method.
- "I do think there's a good framework for thinking. It is physics." [S005]

**Moore's Law (2004).** He borrows the idea as a target metric for launch cost.
- "a constantly improving price-per-pound to orbit, in effect, a Moore's Law of space" [S003]

## Cultural touchstones he uses as shorthand

**Dilbert.** He uses the comic strip as the test for an absurd rule (2018 email) and for his own process mistakes (2021).
- "It was like being in a Dilbert cartoon." [S009]

**Monty Python.** He referenced a Monty Python fish license when a Starship launch waited on wildlife review (inferred from S007).
- "We effectively need a fish license to launch a rocket." [S007]

## Context only, not his words

**Walter Isaacson's biography of Musk.** In S007, Lex Fridman reads a line from this biography about the only rules being the laws of physics. That line is the biographer's framing, read by the host. It is not Musk speaking and is not quoted here as his view. His own closest statement is in frameworks.md, section 3.

## Gaps

The notes name no business, management or economics books he recommends. Do not attribute a reading list to him from these sources.
