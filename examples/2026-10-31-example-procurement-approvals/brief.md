# Brief: cut the purchase approval chain

**Example session (fictional institution).** Round files are omitted to keep the example short; `verdict.md` shows the finished output.

**Decision:** How should we shorten approvals for routine purchases under EGP 50,000?

**Options**
- A. Keep the current 7-signature chain.
- B. Cut to 3 signatures (department head, financial controller, purchasing manager) for all departments now.
- C. Pilot the 3-signature chain with e-approval in two departments (central lab and research core facility) for 90 days, then decide.

**Institution:** public university hospital in Egypt, about 1,200 beds, 4,000 staff, funded by the state budget and university, plus a research center with 300 researchers. The hospital director decides; the university council must approve any change to delegation of authority. (assumed)

**Resources and limits:** no new budget; IT can configure e-approval in the existing ERP within 6 weeks (assumed). Government financial and procurement rules apply. Accreditation survey in 8 months.

**Success:** median approval time for routine purchases from 34 days to under 10 days within 6 months, with no rise in audit findings.

**Leaning:** B, because the delays are hurting research grants with spending deadlines.
