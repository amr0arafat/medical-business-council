# Report template (the Chair's verdict)

Plain English. No em dashes. No filler: state each point once, no tee-ups. Seat quotes keep their own voice. Under about 1,100 words; the round files hold the detail. The reader is a director, dean, or board member who may forward this to a committee, so write for someone who did not see the debate.

```markdown
# Medical Business Council: [decision in a few words]

[date] · Institution: [type, country] · Seats: [list] · Rounds: [n] · Mode: [full | quick | named seats]
Brain mode: [seats] · Lens mode: [seats]

## Verdict: [GO | GO WITH CONDITIONS | PILOT FIRST | RESHAPE | WAIT | DON'T DO IT]

**The call:** [one or two sentences the decision-maker can act on, naming the option]

Option scores: [A] x.x · [B] x.x · [C] x.x · Support for the resolution x.x/10 · Confidence n/10 · Spread n · Vote: [n] endorse, [n] amend, [n] dissent
**Guardian:** [no veto | veto cleared by ... | veto stands: ...]
[Any other override applied, in one line, or omit]
[If the unanimity check added an option: "Option D was added by the Chair after a unanimous Round 1; [seats] were assigned to argue it." Otherwise omit]

## Scorecard

| Seat | Weight | Final stance | [A] | [B] | [C] | Pillar score | Moved? | Their line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Math: [one line per option]

## Why the council landed here

[3 to 5 bullets, each tagged with the seat and framework, e.g. (Grove, production principle)]

## Where they fought

[One short block per crux: the question, who was on each side, who moved and why, how it resolved]

## Governance and compliance

[The Guardian's requirements, each with the rule or standard and the office that signs off. Which are law or accreditation, and which are internal policy the institution can change.]

## Minority report

[The strongest dissent, in the seat's words, and what would have to be true for them to be right. If all agreed, name the least-tested assumption.]

## Conditions

[Each checkable: a number, a date, a test result, or an approval. Mark which must clear before any irreversible step.]

## 30-day plan

1. Week 1: [action] (owner role)
2. Weeks 2 to 4: [action] (owner role)
3. Day 30: [decision checkpoint and who attends]

**Approval path:** [who must sign, in order]
**Cheapest pilot:** [smallest test of the riskiest assumption]
**Success metric:** [the number, its baseline, and its target date]
**Kill criteria:** [the signal that means stop, and what happens then]
**Revisit:** [when to bring this back to the council, with which numbers]

---
Simulated advisors built from each person's public work. They are not the people and do not speak for them. Lens-mode seats paraphrase public frameworks and carry no verified quotes. This is decision support, not legal advice; confirm the governance section with the institution's legal and compliance offices.
```
